## 🚀 4. How to Commission a Custom Project (Step-by-Step)

If your company requires bespoke software development — Autonomous AI Agents, FinTech platforms, Telegram Mini Apps, or high-load web systems:

```
  ┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐
  │    STEP 1    │ ──►  │    STEP 2    │ ──►  │    STEP 3    │ ──►  │    STEP 4    │
  │  Terminal on │      │    Select    │      │ AI Screening │      │ Founder Call │
  │   Website    │      │    Budget    │      │ (Archy Bot)  │      │ in 15 mins   │
  └──────────────┘      └──────────────┘      └──────────────┘      └──────────────┘
```

1. **Step 1: Open the Interactive Terminal**
   - On [induktr.io](https://induktr.io), click **"Initiate Project"** or tap the command prompt interface.
2. **Step 2: Choose Your Scope & Budget Tier**
   - Select your project domain (Autonomous Agents, FinTech, Web Platform, Mobile).
   - Select your budget tier:
     - 🔹 **$3,000 – $5,000** — Fast-track MVP (2–3 weeks), core high-impact features.
     - 🔹 **$5,000 – $10,000** — Full production platform with custom UX and integrations.
     - 🔹 **$10,000+** — Enterprise-grade distributed systems and custom AI infrastructure.
3. **Step 3: Submit Your Contact**
   - Provide your Telegram handle (`@username`) or corporate email.
   - Within 30 seconds, our AI assistant **Archy** structures your requirements and dispatches them to engineering.
4. **Step 4: Receive an Actionable Roadmap within 15 Minutes**
   - The studio founder connects directly in Telegram with a comprehensive breakdown, timeline, and preliminary architecture roadmap.
